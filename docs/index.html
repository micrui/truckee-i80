Interstate 80 through Truckee

An independent public record of the interstate over Donner Pass: how much traffic crosses it, what it carries, what it costs, who governs it, and what happens on it. Built by a Truckee resident from public data (Caltrans detectors, chain-control and closure feeds, and incident records), with every number traceable to its source.

The corridor is one of the busiest mountain freight crossings in the country, and decisions about it are made far from town. This site collects the record in one legible place so local conversations can start from the same facts.

Data this site collects

Collection runs automatically and accumulates in the open in the repository:

  • Traffic volumes and speeds: hourly counts by direction from the 55 Caltrans detector stations on the Truckee–Donner segment, pulled daily from the state's performance measurement system under a registered account.
  • Chain control and closures: every change in chain requirements and lane closures on the corridor, logged as it happens from Caltrans' public feeds.
  • Incidents: collisions, hazards, and vehicle fires on the Truckee–Donner segment, from the daily CHP incident record.

Provenance for every source is documented in DATA-SOURCES.md.

Live conditions (official sources)

  • Caltrans QuickMap: chain controls, closures, cameras, plows, live.
  • Caltrans road information: highway conditions by route number.
  • Caltrans cameras: the Donner Pass camera set.
  • National Weather Service Reno: forecasts for the Sierra crest.
