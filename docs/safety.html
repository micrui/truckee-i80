Accidents and incidents

What actually happens on the corridor, from the CHP's own incident record, collected daily by this site for the mountain segment from Baxter to the Nevada state line, plus the major events of this summer and where the adjudicated crash records live.

February, the deepest winter month, produced 233 incidents; typical summer months run around 140 to 160. Every spinout was logged between January and May: most in January and February, with a late burst in April's spring storms and none after May. No incident this year was dispatch-coded as a fatality; dispatch codes describe the initial call, not final outcomes, so injury severity is only settled in the adjudicated records linked below.

July 20, 2026: a truck lost its brakes near Emigrant Gap

At 1:51 p.m. the CHP logged a collision with ambulances dispatched at the Drum Forebay Road off-ramp, westbound; the entry appears in this site's data for that day. News accounts and official statements describe a commercial truck striking multiple vehicles (around ten by the county's account) before leaving the road and going down an embankment, spilling diesel. Placer County's supervisor for the area attributed the crash to catastrophic brake failure on the truck. Reported injury counts vary: seven per CBS Sacramento, ten (one airlifted) per the county release. No deaths were reported. The westbound freeway closed fully for about six hours, with one lane closed overnight for hazardous-materials cleanup.

Three days later the county convened what it named the Interstate 80 Corridor Safety Initiative, gathering the CHP, Caltrans, fire agencies, the California Trucking Association, and four state legislators, and citing truck brake failures, truck fires, and repeated corridor closures. The supervisor's statement:

These incidents are becoming far too common, and the impacts extend well beyond the crash itself.

Sources: CBS Sacramento · Sierra Sun · Sierra Sun, safety initiative

July 14, 2026: a truck left the road near the summit scales

Six days earlier, at about 3:30 a.m., a solo tractor-trailer crashed eastbound near the CHP commercial vehicle facility east of Truckee. Eastbound traffic was turned around at Overland Trail; the first lane reopened about five hours later. Injuries and cause were not detailed in public coverage.

Sources: CBS Sacramento · KRNV

Winter is the recurring event

The corridor closes, fully or partially, in most major storms. Recent examples: repeated blizzard closures in February–March 2023; a roughly three-day closure of a hundred-mile stretch in the March 2024 blizzard, with dozens of vehicles stranded. This site's chain-control log records every control change and closure on the corridor as it happens.

Sources: AccuWeather, 2023 · KRNV, 2024

The adjudicated records

Dispatch logs are immediate but provisional. The settled crash record (injuries, fatalities, fault, conditions) lives in two public systems: the CHP's Statewide Integrated Traffic Records System, and UC Berkeley's Transportation Injury Mapping System, which geocodes it. Both cover this corridor and are free to query.
